📜 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2025

Your privacy is very important to us. ATS Analyzer is designed to give you control over your resume and job applications while respecting your personal data.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect it.

🔒 Information We Collect

When you use ATS Analyzer, we only process the information you provide directly:

  • 📑 The text from your resume inside Google Docs
  • 📂 The job description text you paste into the add-on

🚫 Information We Do Not Collect

ATS Analyzer does not collect or store the following:

  • 🙅 Personal details like your name, email, phone number, or address
  • 🙅 Files, documents, or job descriptions outside of the active session
  • 🙅 Tracking information, cookies, or browsing history

⚡ How We Use Data

  • 📊 Your resume and job description text are analyzed in real time to generate ATS scores and recommendations
  • 🤖 We use Gemini API (Google’s AI service) to process text securely and return improvement suggestions
  • 🗑️ All processed text is discarded immediately after the analysis is complete — no storage or reuse

🔐 Data Security

  • 🛡️ All processing occurs through Google Docs APIs and Gemini API, both of which follow Google’s strict security standards
  • 🚫 We do not sell, share, or permanently store any resume or job description data
  • ✅ ATS Analyzer complies with Google’s add-on security and data protection requirements

👤 Your Choices

  • ✔️ You can choose not to use the add-on at any time
  • ✔️ You can remove the add-on from your Google account through Google Workspace Marketplace
