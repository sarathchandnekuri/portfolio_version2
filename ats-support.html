Need help? Here are some common questions.

How to install ATS Analyzer?

ATS Analyzer is a Google Docs add-on that evaluates your resume against job descriptions. It highlights missing keywords, gives ATS compatibility scores, and suggests improvements.

Does ATS Analyzer guarantee a job?

No. ATS Analyzer improves your resume’s ATS-friendliness but cannot guarantee interviews or job offers. Results depend on employers and their systems.

How does it calculate ATS scores?

The add-on uses Gemini AI to compare your resume against the job description. It looks for keyword matches, hard skills, soft skills, and formatting alignment.

Do you store my resume or job description?

🚫 No. Your resume and job description are processed in real time and discarded immediately after analysis. We do not store, sell, or share your data.

Do I need to pay to use this tool?

Currently, the add-on is free. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but core resume analysis will remain accessible.

Who is this tool for?

🎓 Students applying for internships or entry-level jobs, 💼 Professionals tailoring resumes for new roles and 🌍 International applicants adjusting resumes for ATS systems.
