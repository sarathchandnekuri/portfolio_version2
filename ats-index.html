AI Resume ATS Analyzer

Applying for jobs today means competing with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
These systems scan resumes before a recruiter ever sees them, and if your resume isn’t optimized, it may never get through.

AI Resume ATS Analyzer is a Google Docs add-on that helps job seekers tailor their resumes for specific job postings.
It scans your resume against a job description and provides clear, actionable insights.

✨ Key Features

  • ✅ AI Resume Optimization – Uses Gemini AI to improve clarity, structure, and relevance
  • 📊 Detailed ATS Score — See how well your resume matches the job description, with a percentage-based score.
  • 🛠 Targeted Suggestions — Get exact recommendations on which sections of your resume need improvements.
  • 🔑 Keyword Matching — Identify missing hard and soft skills that recruiters are looking for.
  • ⚡ Instant Analysis — Run checks directly inside Google Docs in just a few seconds.
  • 🔐 Privacy-Focused – Your data is analyzed in real time and never stored

💼 Why Use ATS Analyzer?

Most companies now rely on ATS software to filter resumes. That means:

  • 📉 Generic resumes often get filtered out
  • 🔍 Recruiters may never see qualified candidates
  • ⏱ Manually tailoring resumes for every job is slow and frustrating

ATS Analyzer makes this process easy, affordable, and reliable.
With every scan, you get insights that can dramatically improve your chances of landing interviews.

🚀 How It Works

  1. 📂 Paste the job description into the add-on’s sidebar
  2. 📑 Run the analysis on your current resume
  3. ✅ Review the ATS score and suggestions provided
  4. ✍️ Update your resume inside Google Docs, guided by the feedback

🔐 Privacy First

  • 🙅 We do not store your resumes or job descriptions
  • ⚡ Analysis happens in real time
  • 🗑️ Data is discarded immediately after use
